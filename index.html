    Questions we get a lot.

    How often should a lawn be mowed in South Florida?

    Weekly from April through October, when St. Augustine and Bahia grow fastest, and every other week in the dry season. Our maintenance plans adjust on their own as the season changes.

    Do you install sod near me?

    Yes, anywhere in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach. We remove the old turf, grade, lay fresh St. Augustine, Zoysia or Bahia, and set your irrigation for the first two weeks of rooting.

    How much does landscaping cost?

    Weekly maintenance usually starts around the price of a dinner out. Design and install projects are quoted after a free walk of the property, with a fixed written price before any work starts.

    Can you fix sprinklers that flood the sidewalk or miss spots?

    Yes. We repair heads, lines and controllers, re-aim coverage, and set the schedule to your county's watering days.

    Do you trim palms and remove trees?

    We trim palms, prune shade trees, treat nutrient problems like frizzle top, and remove trees that are dead or too close to the house.
